                                                             Abstract
Electronic control boards in modern appliances and vehicles are routinely declared unrepairable: a single short-circuited power
semiconductor worth a few tens of rupees leads a technician to recommend replacing an entire motherboard costing thousands. This
paper presents the Right-to-Repair Board Rescuer Deck, a portable diagnostic platform aimed at neighbourhood repair shops (re-
pair-walas) and DIY consumers. The key insight is that failed power semiconductors expose themselves through localised thermal
and electrical signatures when the board is energised at a safe, low voltage. The deck combines a low-cost thermal-imaging array
(MLX90640) with automated multimeter probes in a testing tray, and uses edge AI to localise the faulty device, identify its part
number, retrieve a datasheet, and display a step-by-step desoldering and replacement guide. The approach reduces repair cost from
whole-board replacement to component-level substitution, extending device lifetime and cutting electronic waste.
